Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Summary

Gerrit Hero does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

Data storage

Extension preferences (such as debug mode and feature toggles) are stored locally in your browser via Chrome's storage API. This data never leaves your device.

Data collection

We do not use analytics, tracking, or third-party data services.

Permissions

Gerrit Hero requests access to web pages to detect Gerrit instances and enhance the UI. The extension only activates on pages identified as Gerrit. No page content is sent to external servers.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project's GitHub repository issues.
